Draw Bones is off. Turn on Draw Bones. For a new armature, click Bone 1 head and then its tail. To extend an armature, click an existing bone endpoint and then click the new endpoint. Press Escape or Finish Chain to end the chain. Add Bone creates a child from the selected bone. Extrude uses the selected bone endpoint and lets you click the new endpoint in the 3D view. Edit Joints changes the rest armature. The Name field renames the selected bone. Click and drag a joint, use exact coordinates, or insert, extrude, dissolve, and reparent bones. Individual Cubes keeps every voxel face. Exposed Surface removes hidden internal faces for a smaller Blender scene while retaining bone boundaries. Auto Bind assigns unlocked voxels to the nearest bone segment. Paint Bind assigns voxels in the 3D view. Check Rig reports export problems. Influence Radius controls how far this bone reaches when Smooth Bend calculates automatic weights. The selected bone displays an influence envelope in the 3D view. Weight Paint changes how strongly the selected bone deforms existing voxels and never creates or erases voxels. Draw and select a bone first. Weight is the maximum influence and Strength scales the influence painted by the stroke, so 100 percent Weight with 50 percent Strength paints 50 percent bone influence. Right drag clears painted weights back to automatic influence. Brush is the voxel radius.

Privacy Policy

Pixel Bug keeps your work on your device.

The desktop version uses local app storage and recovery files. The web version uses browser storage on your device.

Projects and images are processed locally. Pixel Bug does not upload your artwork or project data.

The web version is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic connection data needed to load the site.

Files are saved only when you choose a save or export option. In the web version, your browser handles file access and downloads.

Clearing app or browser site storage can remove autosave, gallery, snapshot, and recovery data. Keep separate project files for anything important.
